    Subsection Definitions

    Definition 5.3.1. The Unit Circle.

    A unit circle is a circle with radius 1.
    🔗
    🔗

    Definition 5.3.2. Cosine of an Angle.

    The cosine of an angle, \(\theta\text{,}\) is equal to the \(x\)-value at that angle on a unit circle.
    🔗
    🔗

    Definition 5.3.3. Sine of an Angle.

    The sine of an angle, \(\theta\text{,}\) is equal to the \(y\)-value at that angle on a unit circle.
    🔗
    🔗

    Definition 5.3.4. The Pythagorean Identity.

    The Pythagorean Identity is the relationship between sine and cosine values, \(\sin^2(\theta) + \cos^2(\theta) = 1\text{,}\) relating to the Pythagorean Theorem in [provisional cross-reference: Figure 5.14].
    🔗
    🔗

    Definition 5.3.5. Reference Angle.

    A reference angle, \(\alpha\text{,}\) to an angle \(\theta\text{,}\) is the acute (or right) positive angle between the \(x\)-axis to the angle \(\theta\) as shown in [provisional cross-reference: Figure 5.15].
    🔗
    🔗
