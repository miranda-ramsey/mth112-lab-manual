    Section 5.4 Other Trigonometric Functions

    In this section, we’ll find exact values of the trigonometric functions secant, cosecant, tangent, and cotangent of \(\frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\) \(\frac{\pi}{4}\text{,}\) and \(\frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\) use reference angles to evaluate the trigonometric functions secant, tangent, and cotangent, use properties of even and odd trigonometric functions, recognize and use fundamental identities, and evaluate trigonometric functions with a calculator.
